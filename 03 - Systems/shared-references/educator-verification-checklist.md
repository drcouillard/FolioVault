# Educator Verification Checklist
## Folio Interactive — Reference File
*Alpha → Beta gate. Nothing advances without a named educator sign-off.*

---

## How to Use This Checklist

This checklist is completed by the assigned educator verifier — not by Rob
or Claude. It is the gate between Alpha (functional draft) and Beta
(educator verified, eligible for student pilot).

One checklist per module component submitted for review.
A module may be submitted in parts (artifact, question set, feedback blocks)
or as a complete package. Both are valid. Partial verification is tracked
in the module's content log.
