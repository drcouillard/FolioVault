# Brand Strategy: [Example — Fictional Brand: Fieldwork Coffee Co.]

> **Note:** This is a structural reference only. Used in Step 3 to calibrate output quality. Do not import any values from this example into a real brand session.

---

## One-Line Description
Fieldwork Coffee Co. is a specialty roaster for working professionals who want craft-quality coffee without the pretension.

---

## Mission Statement
To make exceptional coffee accessible — not just affordable, but approachable. We believe the best cup of your day shouldn't require a glossary.

---

## Value Proposition
Fieldwork delivers specialty-grade coffee with straightforward, unpretentious presentation — so people who care about quality don't have to perform their appreciation for it.

---

## Brand Archetype
**Primary:** Explorer
**Secondary:** Everyman

*Rationale: Fieldwork is rooted in discovery — sourcing, roasting, finding the right cup — but it rejects the elitism that often comes with specialty coffee culture. The Everyman secondary keeps it grounded and relatable.*

---

## Target Audience Profiles

**Profile 1 — The Informed Pragmatist**
- 28–42, urban or suburban professional
- Cares about quality but has no patience for affectation
- Already drinks good coffee; skeptical of brands that make it complicated
- Buys on trust and consistency, not trend

**Profile 2 — The Curious Newcomer**
- 22–30, starting to move beyond mass-market coffee
- Interested in specialty but intimidated by the culture around it
- Responds to brands that teach without condescending
- Price-conscious but willing to pay more if the brand earns it

**Profile 3 — The Gift Buyer**
- Buys for others — partners, colleagues, clients
- Needs confidence the product looks credible and feels premium
- Values clean packaging, clear story, easy to explain

---

## Emotional Keywords
Grounded · Honest · Curious · Capable · Unpretentious · Reliable · Alive

---

## Positioning Statement
For working professionals who love quality coffee but hate coffee culture, Fieldwork is the specialty roaster that delivers craft without the attitude — because great coffee should feel like an upgrade to your day, not a test of your knowledge.

---

## Competitive Differentiation
While most specialty roasters compete on origin story and tasting notes, Fieldwork competes on tone. The product quality is there. The difference is that Fieldwork never makes you feel like you're doing it wrong.

---

## Tagline Options
1. *Just good coffee.*
2. *For people who drink coffee, not concepts.*
3. *Find your cup.*

---

## Anti-Positioning
Fieldwork is **not**:
- Pretentious or jargon-heavy
- Trying to be the most elevated option in the room
- Nostalgic or heritage-obsessed
- A lifestyle brand that sells a persona more than a product
