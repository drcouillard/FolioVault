# Folio Interactive — Identity

This note expands the permanent context in `CLAUDE.md` into a single reference for **what Folio is**, **why it exists**, **who it serves**, **how it sounds**, and **how it is positioned** in the market.

---

## What Folio Is

Folio Interactive is an **adaptive K–12 learning platform** delivered as a **responsive web application**. The product personalizes each learner’s path through **adaptive routing**, **targeted remediation** when answers are wrong (not generic feedback), and a **spiral curriculum** so prior ideas keep resurfacing in later practice.

In one line: Folio builds **adaptive educational software** that meets learners where they are, challenges them to think deeply, and gives educators tools that can flex across classrooms and contexts.

---

## Mission

**Mission:** Make rigorous, personalized learning **accessible** — software that **adapts to the learner**, **clarifies the path forward**, and **builds the confidence to keep going**.

Success means learners experience real intellectual demand without being abandoned when they struggle, and adults who support them get trustworthy signals and workflows, not gimmicks.

---

## Founding Principle

**“Could you patent the sun?”** — Jonas Salk.

The **base product is free. Always.** That is **structural**, not a temporary marketing line: pricing and roadmap decisions must preserve a meaningful free core. **AI capabilities** may exist as a **paid enhancement layer**, but the platform must remain **fully usable without AI** and without paywalling the learner’s core path.

---

## Audiences

| Tier | Audience | What they need from Folio |
|------|-----------|----------------------------|
| **Primary** | **K–12 independent learner** — curious, self-motivated | Clear tasks, respectful difficulty, help that teaches (not punishes), and a sense of progress they can trust. |
| **Secondary** | **Classroom educator** | Credibility, outcomes alignment, and deployment that does not add operational chaos. |
| **Tertiary** | **Engaged parent / college learner** | Honest scope, optional depth, and language that does not talk down to adults or kids. |

All messaging and UX choices should default to the **primary** learner first, then **secondary** educator constraints, then **tertiary** contexts.

---

## Brand Personality and Voice

**Personality:** Warm but never soft. Precise but never cold. Speaks like a **teacher who loves the subject**. Never talks down. Treats struggle as **natural** and **diagnostic**, not something to “cheer through” with empty positivity.

**Voice in practice**

- **Prefer:** build, explore, practice, notice, connect, demonstrate, mastery, evidence, reflect.
- **Avoid:** leverage, unlock, crush it, easy, simply, just, fail, optimize, seamless.

**Line in the sand:** **Multimodal by default** (text, visual, interactive where possible) serves accessibility and cognition; Folio does **not** route learners by **VARK** or “learning styles” pseudoscience.

## Positioning

- **Category:** Rigorous, personalized **K–12** learning software — **web-first**, not a native app until **after pilot**.
- **First vertical:** **World Geography**, with **Map Skills** as the **first module** — a concrete wedge that grounds the product in observable skills and rich visuals.
- **Pedagogical spine:** **UDL**, multimodal presentation, **spiral** practice, **adaptive remediation**, and **why-over-rote** questioning — see `CLAUDE.md` for the ordered list and the **three-stage content pipeline** (Draft → Ready for Review → Approved).
- **Quality bar:** **Expert educator verification** before anything ships from draft; nothing is “verified” without a **named educator sign-off**.
- **Operator reality:** Solo founder **Rob** is **not a developer**; execution guidance should favor steps that move Folio toward a **working pilot with real students** without assuming deep engineering literacy unless explicitly scoped.
